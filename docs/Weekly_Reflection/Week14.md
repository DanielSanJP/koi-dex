1. What did I accomplish this week?

Buffer week, so I used it for the two stretch features I had held back, and did them early while the deployment waited on a login.

- Built a pond calculator into the care guide. You enter your pond's measurements (rectangular or round), how many koi you have and their average length, and pick the water temperature. It gives you the volume in litres, how many adult koi that can carry, whether the depth is enough, the pump size you need, the weekly water change in litres, and a rough daily feed amount for that temperature. Every number comes from the figures already checked in the guide, and it says plainly that the feed figure is an estimate.

- Made the koi move. My artwork has 20 hand-drawn fin frames per fin, and until now the site only ever drew the first one. On the koi cards and on each koi's page, hovering or focusing the fish now cycles through the frames over two seconds, the same speed the original renderer uses. It switches itself off if the visitor's system asks for reduced motion.

- Everything still passes the checks: lint, types, the 14 tests, the content validation, and the production build, locally and on GitHub.
