Weekly Progress Update
Submit this form weekly, at least 24 hours before your class session.
Name: Week #:
Student ID: Date:

1. What did I accomplish this week?
   List the specific tasks you worked on or completed — keep it concrete.

2. What do I plan to work on next week?
   Be specific — what are the next 2–3 tasks you’ll tackle?

3. Any challenges or blockers?
   Anything slowing you down or that you need help with? If none, just write “None”.

4. How do I feel about my overall progress?
   Circle or highlight one option below.
   ☐On track ☐Slightly behind ☐Falling behind

Tip: Keeping this updated weekly will make your Mid-Point Review and Final Report much easier to write.
