1. What did I accomplish this week?

This week was the care guide, and it finishes milestone M2 (both guides done and checked).

- Checked every number in the care guide against two separate sources. There are about 30 of them: temperatures, pH, the water chemistry limits, pond size and depth, how often to feed, how big koi get, how long they live, the virus and quarantine numbers. I put the results in a table at the bottom of the care page so a reader can see both sources for each number and whether they agreed.

- Fixed what the check turned up. Some of it was small (ranges a bit too wide or too narrow), but two things were properly wrong. I had the temperature band for ulcer disease at 18 to 29 degrees, and every source I read puts it in cool water, about 5 to 17 degrees, in spring and autumn. And I had written "hobby quarantine is 2 to 4 weeks", and no source I could find said two weeks was enough, so it is now at least four. My feeding table also contradicted my own text: it listed feeds at 5 to 10 degrees while the paragraph next to it said stop feeding below 10. I rebuilt it as six temperature bands.

- Wrote four new sections that were missing: filtration and how a filter actually cycles, water changes (and why topping up is not a water change), how to net and move a koi without hurting it, and predators and why koi jump out. All sourced.

- Added the new pond checklist: 37 things in the order you actually do them, from picking a spot through to the first season. The tick boxes are saved in the browser, so someone building a pond over two months can come back to it.

- Found something I did not expect. Koi are legally a pest in New Zealand: an Unwanted Organism and a noxious fish, and you need approval from DOC to keep them. I checked it on two DOC pages, and it now sits at the top of the care guide because it changes whether a reader here can keep koi at all.

- Got a head start on the plants research for next week, because looking things up properly is the slow part.
