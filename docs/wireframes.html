Koi-dex · wireframes v4 · Koi brand

Koi-dex wireframe deck

Six screens using the real Koi design language from koi-web: Pally type, the sky-blue band with its 3px divider, white cards with the soft card shadow, and the chunky offset-shadow button. The site covers real koi only — the 22 genuine varieties, their care, breeding outcomes, and pond plants — with no game concepts (no rarity, tiers, or glow). Crossed boxes are sprites; orange dots are annotations.
